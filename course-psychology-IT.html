Course Overview

This is not a traditional theory-based psychology class. Students learn through practical activities, role-plays, discussions, real-life simulations, reflection exercises, communication tasks, emotional intelligence training and AI-supported learning. The course helps students understand thoughts, emotions, relationships, behaviour patterns, stress handling and modern digital psychology tools.
