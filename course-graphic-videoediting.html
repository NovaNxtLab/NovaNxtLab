AI Graphic Designing & Video Editing

Course Overview

This course is designed to transform beginners into professional creative editors and designers using industry-standard software, AI tools and modern content creation workflows.

Students will learn social media designing, cinematic editing, motion graphics, branding, AI-generated creatives and portfolio building.

Software & Tools Covered

🎨 Graphic Designing

  • Canva
  • Adobe Photoshop
  • Adobe Illustrator
  • CorelDRAW
  • Figma

🎬 Video Editing

  • CapCut
  • VN Editor
  • Adobe Premiere Pro
  • Adobe After Effects

🤖 AI Tools

  • ChatGPT
  • Runway ML
  • Kling AI
  • Leonardo AI
  • Midjourney
  • ElevenLabs

Course Modules

🎨 Design Fundamentals

Color theory, typography, layout design, visual hierarchy, branding basics & creative thinking.

🖌 Canva Mastery

Social media posts, carousels, thumbnails, presentations, Canva AI & animation features.

📸 Photoshop Mastery

Retouching, manipulation, cinematic grading, AI editing, product ads & professional effects.

✏ Illustrator & CorelDRAW

Logo designing, vector graphics, packaging design & complete branding.

🎥 Video Editing Basics

Timeline workflow, transitions, sync editing, audio balancing & cinematic reels.

🤖 AI Content Creation

AI image generation, AI videos, voiceovers, automation & AI reel creation.
